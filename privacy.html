Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Puzzle 99 Light ("the Application") collects, uses, and protects user information when you use the mobile game.

The Application itself does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or contacts). All game progress, high scores, and local settings are stored exclusively on your device.

The Application uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to serve ads. These third-party services may automatically collect certain technical information to provide relevant advertisements. This information may include:

The collection and processing of this data are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third-party providers. For more details on how Google uses data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal data, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.
